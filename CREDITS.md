# Credits & Attributions

## Lunar imagery
- **Moon texture (`assets/sky/moon.png`)**: Scientific Visualization Studio & Lunar Exploration Photography, [NASA](https://images.nasa.gov) (Public Domain). Sourced via the Heaven on Earth / radar-gh sky assets.

- **Eclipse table (skycal.js)**: "Eclipse Predictions by Fred Espenak, NASA's GSFC" (eclipse.gsfc.nasa.gov, 2021–2030 decade tables). Local visibility is computed on the device.
- **Moon phases, seasons, perihelion/aphelion**: low-precision series after Jean Meeus, *Astronomical Algorithms* (ch. 25, 47).
